Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Scanner ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or use our tools (the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

  • Account data: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Contact form data: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Usage data: We may automatically collect basic technical information such as your browser type, device type, and pages visited, to help us understand how the Service is used.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in securely.
  • To respond to messages submitted through our contact form.
  • To monitor, maintain, and improve the performance and reliability of the Service.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or security issues.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the Service — such as our database and hosting provider, and our email delivery provider — solely for the purpose of running the Service and delivering contact form messages to us.

5. Third-Party Data Sources

The Service generates signals using publicly available cryptocurrency market data. This does not involve sharing your personal information with any data provider.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. Messages submitted through the contact form are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and for our internal records.

7. Cookies

We may use minimal, functional cookies or local storage to remember basic preferences, such as display settings. We do not use these to build advertising profiles.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data or any information you have submitted to us through the contact form.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out using the contact form on our homepage.
