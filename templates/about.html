We built the scanner we wished existed.

Pro Scanner exists to remove the manual, repetitive part of technical analysis — checking coin after coin for the same setup — and hand you a clean, ranked list instead.

Our story

Pro Scanner began as a personal tool: a script to check whether the coins on a watchlist matched a specific RSI/MACD/moving-average setup before every trading session. As the watchlist grew into "every USDT-M futures pair," the script grew into a proper scanning engine — one that runs the same logic consistently, without fatigue, bias, or missed coins.

Today that engine powers the Signals page on this site: it pulls live 4-hour, 1-hour and 15-minute candles for the top pairs by volume, checks trend and momentum, optionally confirms with candlestick and chart-pattern recognition, and only surfaces setups that clear a high confidence bar.

What we believe

Trading tools should show their work. Every signal on Pro Scanner traces back to a specific, inspectable rule — not a black box. We'd rather show you fewer setups that meet a clear bar than flood you with noise.

◆

Consistency

The same rules are applied to every coin, every scan — no favorites, no fatigue.

◈

Transparency

Every signal shows the entry, stop, target and the exact pattern that triggered it.

◇

Control

Pick how many pairs to scan and run it whenever you want — the same full confirmation logic every time.

A tool, not a promise

No scanner can predict the market. Pro Scanner is a technical-analysis tool that highlights setups matching defined rules — it does not guarantee outcomes, and it isn't financial advice. Risk management is always on you.
